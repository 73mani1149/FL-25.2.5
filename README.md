## 🎓 Getting Started with Music Production

New to music production? Here's how to start your first project:

1. Open FL-25.2.5
2. Select a template - the "Basic" template is a good starting point
3. Click the step sequencer (the window with pattern blocks)
4. Click on drum sound slots to add beats
5. Press play to hear your pattern

From there, you can add melodies with the piano roll and structure your song in the playlist view. The interface may seem complex at first, but you'll quickly understand the workflow through experimentation.
